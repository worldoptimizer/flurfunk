Tasks

A board for everything that has to get done.

Any thread can be a task. The board shows the tasks you can see in four columns; a click opens the thread in a drawer over the columns.

  • Drag a card to change its state, or press Alt+Arrow on a focused card.
  • Question notifies the person who started the task and the responsible people.
  • Due dates remind people once a day at the hour they chose.
  • Filter by channel and by responsible person; Done keeps the last 14 days.
