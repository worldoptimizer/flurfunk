AI assistants

An assistant that works with you, not past you.

Mention an assistant in a thread, or talk to it in private. It reads what you may read, writes drafts if you let it, and suggests changes to posts as one card you apply or undo. WordPress 7.0 or newer, with the AI provider your site already has.

  • Never more rights than the person asking, and it never administers the site. A role can limit it further.
  • Drafts with you as the author, images from the conversation included. It never publishes.
  • Corrections for several posts as one card with before and after: untick, apply, undo.
  • Follow-ups without a new mention. A thanks gets a reaction, not another message.
  • Off by default, a monthly budget per assistant, a log without content.
