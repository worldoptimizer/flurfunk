Notifications

Told about what concerns you. Left alone otherwise.

Mentions, tasks, due dates and replies in your threads reach you in the app, by mail and by Web Push. Each person decides how much.

  • Mail right away, as one daily summary, for everything, or never.
  • Web Push to desktop browsers, Android, and iPhone home screen apps.
  • Quiet hours, a pause button, and per channel: as usual, every new post, or only mentions.
  • Follow, mute or “remind me” on any thread.
