What it does

Conversations next to the work, not in another tool.

Flurfunk lives in wp-admin. Threads sit next to the posts they are about, tasks have a board, and everyone gets told what concerns them.

    Channels and threads

    A thread has a title, replies, files and cards. Attach it to a post and it shows up in the block editor’s Conversations panel and the posts list.

    Tasks and a board

    Any thread can be a task: Open, In progress, Question or Done, with a due date and up to 20 responsible people. Move cards by drag and drop or with Alt+Arrow keys.

    Files, media library, gallery

    Up to 50 files per message, uploaded or picked from the media library. Images become a gallery with a lightbox; uploads only enter the media library when someone adds them.

    Cards from other plugins

    Type / for commands. /post inserts a post as a card, plugins add their own. A card keeps a snapshot of its data and can be refreshed on request.

    A full-screen web app

    /flurfunk/ shows the app without admin bar or menu. Add it to the home screen on iPhone or Android, and push opens the thread right there.

    Team and guests

    Each channel says what the team and what guests see: everything, only threads about their own posts, or nothing. Guest authors talk about their texts without seeing internal discussions.

    Your data, your tables

    Own database tables, no external service. One JSON format, flurfunk/1, for export and import with or without files. Works with WordPress’s privacy export and erase tools.
